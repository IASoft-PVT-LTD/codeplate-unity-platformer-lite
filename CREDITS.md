# Credits

Built with CodePlate (codeplate.io)

Every image, sound, music loop and the pixel font in this template are original placeholders made for CodePlate by its placeholder generator (drawn and synthesized from scratch in code). They're listed in `assets.json` with `license: CodePlate-original` and are free to ship in games made from this template.

No third-party assets are included.

The game shows its credits from `Assets/Resources/Content/credits.txt` (title > Credits). Keep its line "Built with CodePlate (codeplate.io)": the free license (`LICENSE.md`) asks for it.

When you add assets, list each one here with its author, license and source link, set its `license` and `credit` in `assets.json`, and add a line to `Assets/Resources/Content/credits.txt`.
